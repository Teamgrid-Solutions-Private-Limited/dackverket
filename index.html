What we believe

Successful tyre workshops are built by engaged people.

How we think

The entrepreneur on the floor knows what works. The workshops we choose to work with are already well-run and respected in their local markets. Our role is to stand alongside the owner and ask: what do you need to thrive and grow from here?

Each workshop continues exactly as before. Same name. Same team. What changes is that the owner no longer carries it alone, but as part of a group of peers who understand what it means to have built something from the ground up.

Something we consistently hear from owners who have joined is that they have found new energy. Carrying a business alone for many years takes its toll. As a partner in Däckverket you remain your own boss, but you do so in a context with peers who understand what it means to have built something from the ground up.

Däckverket is an owner group with a deep commitment to entrepreneurship in the tyre industry. Each workshop operates independently, backed by an industrial platform.

How we work

Four principles

Four principles that guide our approach to every workshop we meet.

01 · Autonomy

You continue to lead

We come in as owners; you remain in charge. All decisions about day-to-day operations stay local, with you and your team.

02 · Focus

Deep expertise in what we do

Our focus is tyre service and adjacent services. This makes us concrete and effective. We can go deep on the issues that genuinely affect your business every day.

03 · Partnership

You become a co-owner

When you sell your workshop to us, you become a co-owner of Däckverket. We build this together, and the value growth is shared by all.

04 · Selection

We choose with care

The owners and workshops that join Däckverket are already among the strongest in their markets. That is a prerequisite, and a strength for the entire group.

Workshops

Our workshops

Däckverket's workshops are located across Sweden, from the north to Skåne in the south, each with deep local roots and long experience of their craft. The work spans everything from passenger car tyres to heavy contracting, and from seasonal changes to advanced wheel alignment. The core of Däckverket is tyre service. The group also includes workshops with auto mechanical services, a natural complement that mirrors how many of our partners work with their customers. Behind every service stands an owner who knows their market, their customers and their workshop inside out.

The founders

Why we founded Däckverket

The Swedish tyre service market is fragmented. It holds stable, profitable businesses with loyal customers and strong local roots, but lacks structures for shared development and natural ownership transitions. Lennart Walerud and Dariush Ghatan identified the opportunity and analysed the conditions thoroughly: the economics of the industry, which ownership model would attract the right workshops, and how a group could be built to create long-term value.

The conclusion was Däckverket. Founded in 2023.
