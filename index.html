About us

What is Däckverket?

"It has been important for me to continue running my business operationally, while receiving support and working together on ownership matters as a co-owner in Däckverket."

— Pelle Eriksson, Tälje Däckservice

We believe in the power of entrepreneurship

Däckverket is an ownership group of committed entrepreneurs operating high-quality, well-managed tire workshops across Sweden. As entrepreneurs, we run our workshops autonomously while collaborating to leverage each other's experience and strengths.

Däckverket is not a chain or franchise, but an ownership group with great passion for entrepreneurship in the tire industry. Our workshops remain free to operate independently — backed by an industrial platform.

Those of us in Däckverket are active co-owners of the whole. We continue to lead our workshops as usual, but with the support of each other and our network of partners.

Background

Why did we start Däckverket?

This business was taken over by my father in the 70s and has been in the family for 50 years. You don't want just anyone to take over. That's why Däckverket was a perfect solution.

— Håkan Berg, Däckarna i Mellerud

We started Däckverket from a simple insight: together we are stronger. Running a business long-term requires the strength of being bigger — without losing the power of entrepreneurship.

Our goal is clear. We want to be the preferred choice for workshop owners who think long-term locally, while also wanting to be part of a shared growth journey.

The Swedish tire service market is fragmented with hundreds of independent workshops and a demographic wave of generational ownership shifts. That creates a unique opportunity to build a leading Nordic player through organic growth and selective acquisitions.
