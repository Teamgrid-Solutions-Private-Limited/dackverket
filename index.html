Why did we start Däckverket?

This business was taken over by my father in the 70s and has been in the family for 50 years. You don't want just anyone to take over. That's why Däckverket was a perfect solution.

— Håkan Berg, Däckarna i Mellerud

We started Däckverket from a simple insight: together we are stronger. Running a business long-term requires the strength of being bigger — without losing the power of entrepreneurship.

Our goal is clear. We want to be the preferred choice for workshop owners who think long-term locally, while also wanting to be part of a shared growth journey.

The Swedish tire service market is fragmented with hundreds of independent workshops and a demographic wave of generational ownership shifts. That creates a unique opportunity to build a leading Nordic player through organic growth and selective acquisitions.
